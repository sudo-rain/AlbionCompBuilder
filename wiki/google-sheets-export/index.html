Export & import

Share a finished composition with your guild and bring one back into the app. Albion Comp Builder supports a full round-trip: export to Google Sheets or to an .xlsx file, and import a composition from an .xlsx file.

Export to Google Sheets

Choose Export to Google Sheets on a composition. The first time, you'll sign in with Google and grant access so the app can create a sheet on your behalf. Then pick or create a worksheet, and the composition is written out for sharing.

Export to .xlsx

Prefer a file? Export the composition to an .xlsx spreadsheet you can store, send, or open in Excel or Google Sheets yourself.

Importing an .xlsx

To bring a composition back in, use Import and choose an .xlsx file that was exported from Albion Comp Builder. The app reads the loadouts back into a composition you can edit. Importing a sheet that matches the export format is the round-trip counterpart to exporting — a comp you exported can be re-imported later.
