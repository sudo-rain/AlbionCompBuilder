Export & import

Share a finished composition with your guild and bring one back into the app. Albion Comp Builder supports a full round-trip: export to Google Sheets or to an .xlsx file, and import a composition from an .xlsx file.

Export to Google Sheets

Choose Export to Google Sheets on a composition. The first time, you'll sign in with Google and grant access so the app can create a sheet on your behalf. Each export creates a new sheet in an Albion Loadout Planner folder in your Google Drive, ready for sharing.

Signing in to Google

The first time you export to Google Sheets, the app starts a one-time sign-in. A browser window opens to Google's sign-in page; once you approve access the app can proceed. After that, your credentials are stored in the system keychain and future exports skip this step entirely.

The access granted covers only files the app creates — it cannot read or modify anything else in your Drive.

If sign-in is interrupted

If you close the browser tab before approving, or the sign-in stalls, the composition view shows a waiting banner: Google sign-in is waiting. Sign-in expires in 5 minutes. From the banner you can:

  • Open Google sign-in — reopens the sign-in page in your browser so you can try again without restarting the export.
  • Cancel upload — abandons the current export attempt. The app shows a brief dismissible notice; your composition data is never affected.

Cancelling is always safe. It only stops that export — you can choose Export to Google Sheets again whenever you are ready.

What gets exported

Each export creates a new Google Sheet in an Albion Loadout Planner folder in your Drive. The sheet is named after your composition with a date and time stamp, so repeated exports produce separate versioned files rather than overwriting previous ones.

Once the upload completes, the sheet opens automatically in your browser and a green Opened in Google Sheets banner appears with an Open sheet link in case you need it later.

Export to .xlsx

Prefer a file? Export the composition to an .xlsx spreadsheet you can store, send, or open in Excel or Google Sheets yourself.

Importing an .xlsx

To bring a composition back in, use Import and choose an .xlsx file that was exported from Albion Comp Builder. The app reads the loadouts back into a composition you can edit. Importing a sheet that matches the export format is the round-trip counterpart to exporting — a comp you exported can be re-imported later.
