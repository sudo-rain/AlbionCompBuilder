Getting Started

Albion Comp Builder is a desktop app for planning Albion Online ZvZ party compositions offline — with full game data, spell options, and one-click export to share with your guild. This page walks you through building your first composition end to end.

1. Create a composition

From the Dashboard, click New Composition, give it a name, and confirm. A composition is a collection of loadouts — one per role or player slot in your comp.

2. Add a loadout and pick a role

Inside the composition, add a loadout and choose its role (Main Tank, Healer, DPS, and so on). The role colors the loadout so the comp is easy to read at a glance.

3. Fill slots in the builder

Open the loadout to enter the three-pane builder: the slot grid on the left, the item browser in the center, and ability detail on the right. Select a slot, pick an item, and its abilities and stats appear instantly. See Building a composition for the full tour.

4. Export and share

When the comp is ready, export it to Google Sheets or to an .xlsx file to share with your guild. See Export & import.
