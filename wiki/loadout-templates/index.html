Loadout templates

A template is a reusable role loadout — for example "Main Tank", "Healer", or "DPS" — that you build once and apply across many compositions. Build your standard roles a single time and drop them into every rally.

Saving a loadout as a template

From a loadout you've built, choose Save as template, give it a name, and confirm. The template captures the gear, spells, and role so you can reuse the exact setup later.

[Screenshot: Save as template — add asset]

Applying a template

When creating or editing a loadout, open the Template Picker and select a template to apply its gear and spells to that loadout. From there you can tweak the result without affecting the saved template.

[Screenshot: Template Picker — add asset]

Managing templates

The Loadout Templates view lists all your saved templates. Open one to edit it, rename it, or delete templates you no longer use. You can also select several templates at once to bulk edit them.
